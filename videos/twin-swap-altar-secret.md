## Topic

To save her family from disgrace, a woman takes her disfigured twin sister's
place at the altar on the day of an arranged marriage to the Alpha King —
expecting him to reject her the moment he realizes which sister actually
showed up. She goes through the ceremony braced for public humiliation, for
the exile that's supposed to follow, for anything but this: he looks at her
like he already knows exactly who she is, and marries her anyway, without a
word of protest. It isn't a mistake. He asked for her, specifically, and let
the court believe the arrangement was for her sister all along, because the
truth of why would have started a war. Now she has to live inside a marriage
built on a secret only he understands, surrounded by a court that believes
she stole a place that was never meant for her — and find out what he
actually knows about her before someone else uncovers it first.
