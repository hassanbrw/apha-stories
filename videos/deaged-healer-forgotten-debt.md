## Topic

A healer in her sixties has spent decades quietly tending to a court that
barely remembers her, including the young pack heir she once saved during a
losing battle when he was barely more than a boy — the one debt the current
Alpha King has never stopped trying to repay. When a reversal spell she casts
to save a dying patient tears through her own body instead, she wakes up in
the frame of a woman in her twenties, with no proof of who she is and no way
to explain it that anyone at court would believe. Forced to start over as an
unknown healer with no name anyone recognizes, she's assigned to the Alpha
King's own chambers after a poisoning nearly kills him — and finds herself
falling for the very man she once bandaged as a boy, while he searches
obsessively for the old healer he owes his life to, never once suspecting the
stranger at his bedside has already saved him twice.
