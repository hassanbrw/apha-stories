## Topic

To fend off a political marriage his court is pressuring him into, the Alpha
King asks a low-ranked servant girl to pose as his fiancée for a single
season — strictly temporary, strictly business, no one is supposed to feel
anything real. She agrees because it buys her family protection they
desperately need. The arrangement is going exactly to plan, cold and
convincing enough to fool the court, until the night of their engagement
ball, when an assassination attempt targets the Alpha King in the middle of
the celebration. She is the one who sees it coming a half-second before
anyone else and puts herself between him and the blade. In the chaos and
aftermath, what was supposed to be a performance stops being one for both of
them — and now a political marriage nobody was ever supposed to take
seriously has real enemies who want it to fail.
