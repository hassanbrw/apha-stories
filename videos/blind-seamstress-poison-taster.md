## Topic

A blind seamstress in the Alpha King's court is the only one who can sense
that he is slowly being poisoned — not by sight, but by the faintest wrongness
in the taste of his food, a sensitivity she's had since childhood. When she
tries to warn him, no one believes a blind servant over the King's own
trusted cook. Desperate, she starts secretly tasting his meals before they
reach him, risking her own life meal after meal. The Alpha King, cold and
guarded, begins to notice: she is always near his food, always a half-second
too careful. When he finally corners her about it, expecting treachery, he
instead finds the reason no one else caught the poison at all — and can't
understand why a servant would risk dying for a King who never showed her a
single kindness. As the poisoning intensifies and the true culprit (someone
close to the throne) grows desperate, he pulls her out of the servants'
quarters and keeps her at his side, telling himself it's only to keep her
tasting his food safely. It stops being only that.
