## Topic

Every full moon, the Alpha King locks himself in a cellar beneath the
castle so that no one has to see what he becomes when his wolf takes over
completely — not a controlled shift, but something raw and violent that he
has spent years hiding from his own court, convinced that if anyone saw it
they would think him a monster unfit to rule. A widowed healer, brought in
years ago to treat him after one especially brutal night left him wounded
and bleeding, is the only person who has ever gone into that cellar during
the change and come out again. She doesn't flinch from the howling, doesn't
recoil from what he becomes — she simply stays, speaks low and steady until
the worst of it passes, and treats his wounds afterward without a single
word of fear or judgment. Over years of these full-moon nights, something
quiet and real has grown between them, built entirely in the dark, in a
version of himself he has never let anyone else witness. The rest of the
court still sees only the composed, controlled Alpha King by daylight, with
no idea that the one person who has seen him at his absolute worst is the
same servant they barely notice in the halls. The danger arrives when the
council, pressured by a rival pack that questions the King's fitness to
rule, demands proof that the Alpha King's wolf is fully under control — an
old law requiring a public demonstration during the next full moon, in
front of witnesses, to settle the matter once and for all. He is certain
the demonstration will destroy him: either he suppresses the change enough
to fake control and risks the wolf breaking free mid-performance in front
of the entire court, or he shows them the truth and confirms every fear
they have about him. The healer is the only one who has ever actually seen
what happens and lived to describe it accurately, and as the noble pushing
for the demonstration realizes she could either save the King's reputation
or destroy it with her testimony, she becomes a target the King has never
had to protect her from before, forcing him to decide whether to keep
hiding what he is or finally let the woman who has never been afraid of him
stand beside him in the light, in front of everyone, exactly as she always
has in the dark.
