## Topic

Twelve years ago, the Ashwood pack was slaughtered in a single night — every
noble, every guard, every heir, or so the surviving kingdoms believed. The
official story blamed a rival territory dispute; the truth, buried since,
was an inside betrayal that let the attackers through the gates. One child
survived: the infant heir, smuggled out in a laundry cart by a dying
servant and raised in secret, moved from household to household, never
told her real name, passed off as an orphaned kitchen girl wherever she
landed. She has spent her whole life small and unnoticed on purpose,
because the people who ordered the massacre are still alive, still
powerful, and still watching for any sign the Ashwood bloodline didn't
die with the rest of them. Alpha King Corwin has spent years quietly
searching for that lost heir — not out of duty alone, but because the
Ashwood territory was his own family's closest ally before the massacre,
and its rightful heir returning would finally let him expose who was
really behind that night and hold them accountable, something the
politics of his own court have blocked him from doing without proof or a
legitimate claimant to stand behind. The trail goes cold every time. Then
a minor lord holds a mocking mating auction — a cruel joke dressed up as
tradition, meant to humiliate an unclaimed servant girl nobody wants by
selling her off for the lowest possible price in front of a laughing
crowd. She is sold for a single copper coin. Corwin is only there as a
political courtesy to the lord hosting it, half paying attention, until
something about the girl on the platform stops him cold — a birthmark, a
turn of phrase, a scar in the shape of something he was told to look for
years ago in an old description he half-memorized and never gave up on.
He buys nothing, says nothing that night, but he does not let her out of
his sight again. As he gets close enough to confirm what his instincts
already know, the people who orchestrated the massacre twelve years ago
realize their carefully buried secret is still breathing, still walking
around in front of the one Alpha King with both the motive and the power
to unbury it — and they decide that finishing what they started twelve
years ago is safer than letting her live long enough to remember who she
really is.
