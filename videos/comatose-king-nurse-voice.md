## Topic

A war-orphaned nurse with no family and no standing in the pack is assigned to
the night ward where the Alpha King lies in a coma after a battlefield wound
that should have killed him. He hasn't woken in weeks, hasn't responded to
his own Beta, his mother, or the healers the court has brought in one after
another. On her very first night alone with him, she talks to him the way
she talks to every patient — softly, honestly, about nothing important — and
his hand moves. Over the following nights, he responds only to her: a
twitch, a breath, a single word, always when her voice is the one in the
room and never for anyone else. When he finally wakes, he doesn't remember
the battle, doesn't fully recognize his own court, but he knows her voice
instantly and asks for it before he asks for anything else — and now a
council that has never noticed her existence has to explain who she is,
while she has to explain a bond she doesn't understand herself and can't
prove she didn't engineer.
