## Topic

Every bride the Alpha King has taken has died within a week of completing
the mating bond — six wives, six deaths, all within seven days, and no one
in the kingdom can explain why. The pack has started calling him cursed;
some whisper he is a monster who kills his own mates, others whisper the
bond itself is broken in him. When a seventh marriage is arranged — this
time to a laundress with no family left to grieve her and nothing left to
lose — she goes into it already resigned to dying within the week, the way
the others did. But she survives the first night. Then the third day. Then
the week passes, and she is still alive, and no one — least of all the
Alpha King himself — can explain why she is different from the six who came
before her. He starts watching her too closely, half-expecting to see
whatever killed the others finally catch up to her, and slowly realizes he
is not just watching her survive — he is watching her live, in a way none of
the others ever got the chance to. The danger comes when the court begins
digging into why the previous six actually died: it was never a curse on
him at all, but a slow poison administered by someone close to the throne
who benefits from an unstable, wifeless Alpha King — and the poison was
always timed to the mating bond itself, meaning the seventh bride survived
only because of some accidental, unknown resistance in her own body, and
whoever has been killing his wives for years is about to realize their
method finally failed, and try something more direct before the truth comes
out.
